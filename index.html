Why we exist

A new pair of shoes changes how a kid walks into a room.

Too many kids start the school year in shoes that are worn through or two sizes too small — and a new pair lifts that weight off a parent, too. Solemates meets kids where they already are, through the schools and community groups they trust, with shoes and books that are theirs to keep.

Our story

It started with one kid who needed shoes.

"I got teased as a kid for the worn-out sneakers I had to wear. Solemates is about making sure no kid has to feel that."

From that experience, Kevin Holmes built Gift of Sole in Kansas City and grew it to thousands of pairs and hundreds of thousands raised. Solemates Collective carries that mission forward in Minnesota — same heart, new name, and now books too.

Kevin HolmesFounder · Anchor & Reporter, KARE 11
